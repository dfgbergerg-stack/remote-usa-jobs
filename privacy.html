Information we collect

Information you give us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, when you visit we (and the services listed below) may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, résumé or payment details, and you never need an account.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Our site itself does not need cookies to work, but the analytics and advertising services we use may set cookies or use similar technologies (such as device identifiers) to count visits, prevent fraud, and show and measure ads.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, the site still works, but ads may be less relevant.

Third-party services we use

  • Web hosting. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to run and secure the site.
  • Analytics – Histats (histats.com). Helps us understand how many people visit and which pages are popular. It may use cookies.
  • Advertising. We show ads from third-party advertising networks (ad scripts are loaded from bauval.org). Ad partners may use cookies, web beacons and IP addresses to serve ads, limit how often you see them and measure performance. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Saved jobs and offline pages. Jobs you save and pages you visit may be stored in your own browser (local storage and the browser cache) so they open instantly and work offline. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us; you can clear it anytime in your browser settings.

How we use information

  • To run, maintain and secure the website.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To show advertising, which keeps the site free.
  • To reply to your emails and handle requests such as listing removals or DMCA notices.
  • To prevent abuse, spam and fraud.

How information is shared

We don’t sell the information you send us by email. Information is shared only with the service providers described above, when required by law, or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

When you click “Apply” or any link to another website, you leave our site. That website’s own privacy policy applies to anything you share there, including job applications.

Your advertising choices

You can opt out of interest-based advertising from many ad companies here:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance (US)
  • Network Advertising Initiative
  • Your Online Choices (EU/UK)

You can also block third-party cookies in your browser settings.

Data retention

Emails are kept only as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of requests (such as removal or DMCA requests). Server logs and analytics data are kept for the periods set by those providers.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the site and the little information we hold, but no website or internet transmission is 100% secure.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.

To make a request, email contact@remoteusajobs.up.railway.app with the subject “Privacy request”. We will respond within 30 days. For data collected by our analytics or advertising partners, you can also use the opt-out links above or contact those providers directly. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for adults looking for work. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact contact@remoteusajobs.up.railway.app and we will delete it.

International visitors

Our site and service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, where data protection laws may differ.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email contact@remoteusajobs.up.railway.app.
